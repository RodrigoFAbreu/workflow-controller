## The Controller-run Codex review accepts codex-cli 0.163.0 (1.22.2)

**The problem.** The Controller-run Codex external review (`reviewer.harness=codex`) was refused at launch on the
installed `codex-cli 0.163.0`. Its `codex features list` reports three features enabled that were in neither the
allowlist (`TOOL_ALLOWLIST`) nor the disabled list (`DISABLED_FEATURES`): `api_key_cyber_access_programs`, `cli_daybreak`
and `code_mode_interrupt` (#168).

**What changes.** All three features are now disabled, unconditionally, with `--disable`, which 0.163.0 accepts. None is
in the allowlist. The review argv carries three more `--disable` pairs.

**The pin.** `PINNED_VERSION`, the Codex adapter's `pinned_version`, the fake Codex's version and its default feature
capture all move to 0.163.0. The capture `features_list.0.162.1.txt` is replaced by `features_list.0.163.0.txt`. The
other captures and their digests are kept.

**What was re-measured.** Only the feature list and the acceptance of the three `--disable` names were re-measured on
0.163.0. The real `codex exec` runs and the sandbox probe were not repeated: they remain the measurements on 0.162.1, and
the README of `tests/harness_contract/codex/` says so.

**A later update.** A later Codex update that changes a checked surface still fails closed at launch, naming the check and
the feature. The durable fix is `verify-codex` (C10g-2) and H7.

**Unchanged.** Enabling `reviewer.harness=codex` stays the operator's settings change. There is no new setting, exit code,
step count or gate identity.

**The documentation.** The [runtime guide](../guide/runtime.md), the [harnesses guide](../guide/harnesses.md) and
[common problems](../common-problems.md) describe the pin and the refusal.
