**State-writer discipline (D1, item 354):** every `docs/ai-workflow/WORKFLOW_STATE.json` write this command performs -- everywhere a step below says "persist the returned state" -- is performed by calling `workflow_state.state_transaction(repo_root, mutator)`, never by a separate read-then-write: `state_transaction` holds `.ai-review/runtime/WORKFLOW_STATE.lock` (`workflow_state.state_lock`, `fcntl.flock(LOCK_EX)`) across the complete re-read -> apply-the-named-function -> canonical-serialize -> atomic-publish sequence in one process invocation, so `mutator` is the exact transition function each step below names (e.g. `lambda state: workflow_state.<fn>(state, ...)`), applied to freshly re-read state rather than to a snapshot taken before the lock was acquired.

Adopt the repository's gate policy (`workflow-2.8.0`, `gate-policy-and-reopening`,
D-GP-Policy, `docs/ai-workflow/GATE_POLICY.md`). This command is **work-item-free**:
it names no work item, touches no work item's entry and no approval stage, and it
is not a state of `MILESTONE_WORKFLOW.md`.

**This command is user-only by construction**, the same authority shape
`/approve-review` and `/request-plan-amendment` use. `disable-model-invocation:
true` is the primary, harness-enforced control. Claude must never invoke it on the
user's own behalf, including as a step of another command's execution,
independent of that flag.

Why it exists: the effective policy is, field by field, the **stricter** of the
last adopted policy, the recorded floor and `docs/ai-workflow/GATE_POLICY.json`.
A file can therefore only ever tighten the policy. Adoption is the user's act of
loosening it, and the only one: it records the file's policy body in the state
file's top-level `gate_policy_adoption` and resets `gate_policy_floor` to that
policy's resolved form.

**User-only guard**: refuse to write anything unless the user's own current-turn
message supplies confirmation text containing **both** the literal `gate_policy`
and the first 12 hexadecimal characters of the digest shown in step 1
(`workflow_state.validate_gate_policy_confirmation(text, digest)`). It names no
work item and no approval stage and never touches `validate_user_confirmation` or
`APPROVAL_STAGES`. Never fabricate, infer or carry over a confirmation from a
prior turn; if it is missing, show step 1 and stop and wait.

1. **Show what would be adopted, then stop for the confirmation.** Call
   `workflow_state.gate_policy_adoption_preview(repo_root)` and print, in full:
   - the file's digest and its first 12 hexadecimal characters (the prefix the
     confirmation must quote), and the policy body;
   - the policy in effect now without the file (`current`, its `source` and
     digest) and the resolved difference: each **loosening** (`loosened`) and
     each **tightening** (`tightened`) the adoption makes;
   - the floor it resets (`floor_reset_to`), and the `lowered` label it will
     record -- a non-empty `lowered` makes the adoption a **gate-lowering
     event**, which `verify` and `next-action` report and every automatic
     satisfaction copies into its audit record;
   - every open plan-stage and implementation-stage bundle of **any** work item
     that the adoption commit will stale (`stales`), with the way out of each.
     `HEAD` is repository-wide, so the adoption commit moves it past every open
     bundle's `generation_head`: a plan-stage bundle is withdrawn and
     regenerated with `/milestone-plan <id>` (both review stages are then
     recorded again), an implementation-stage bundle is recovered with
     `/recover-implementation-provenance <id>` from the phases that command
     admits, and otherwise regenerated by the item's own remediation cycle.
     Adopting while a bundle is open is allowed, it is the user's act; it is
     **not** a remedy for a blocked gate at an open review phase.

   `GatePolicyFileInvalidError` (the file is absent, or invalid -- `exc.errors`
   lists why) stops here: report it and the file's required shape from
   `docs/ai-workflow/GATE_POLICY.md`. `GatePolicyFileUncommittedError` (a
   subclass: the working-tree file differs from `HEAD`'s copy) stops here too:
   the adoption commit stages only the state file, so an uncommitted file would
   be reported adopted while the committed policy (and its floor) stayed in
   force, with a false gate-lowering event. Tell the user to **commit the file,
   then run `/adopt-gate-policy`** again; never commit it on their behalf. A `provenance_ok: false` preview is
   reported as well: the adoption is then the way out (it becomes the newest
   change to both fields and the lower bound of the floor range).
2. **Adopt.** Only with the confirmation in hand, call
   `workflow_state.adopt_gate_policy(repo_root, confirmation=<text>, now=<now>)`.
   It validates the confirmation against the digest, **refuses a non-empty index
   apart from `docs/ai-workflow/WORKFLOW_STATE.json`** (`DirtyIndexBeforeStagingError`
   naming the staged paths: the commit takes the whole index, and a user's own
   staged paths would otherwise be swept into a commit its validator can only
   reject after it landed), records the adoption and the reset floor with
   `workflow_state.record_gate_policy_adoption` inside one `state_transaction`,
   and stages them with `workflow_state.stage_scoped_state(repo_root,
   workflow_state.GATE_POLICY_ADOPTION_SCOPE)`: only the two top-level keys
   `gate_policy_adoption` and `gate_policy_floor` go on top of `HEAD`'s state, so
   another work item's uncommitted residue is neither committed nor refused
   (without such residue the ordinary single-path `git add` runs and the bytes
   are the unscoped result). It creates **one** commit carrying
   `Workflow-Gate-Policy-Adoption: <digest>` as the message's own final paragraph
   -- after any `Co-Authored-By:`/`Claude-Session:` lines, never before them
   (`OPUS-R129-001`) -- and no other `Workflow-*` trailer, then runs
   `workflow_state.validate_gate_policy_adoption_commit`, whose contract is
   exactly the two top-level keys and no work-item change.
3. **Report** the commit sha, the digest, the `lowered` fields (or that it lowers
   nothing), and the effective policy and its `source` afterwards
   (`workflow_gate_policy.effective_policy`). An adoption **keeps verifying after
   a squash merge**: both fields are verified by their content and the chain of
   their changes, never by the commit message. Two branches that each adopt from
   the same base conflict in their chains; the second **fails closed** (every
   gate human, `source: provenance_failed`), and the remedy is to take
   `main`'s adoption record on the update merge and run this command again.
